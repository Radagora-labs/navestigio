Follow the traces left by whoever browsed.

Navestigio is a free browser forensics tool. It analyzes history, downloads, cookies and saved credentials from Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari in a single page that works offline. The evidence never leaves the computer and is never modified.

The questions of a case, in one tool

Open the profile files and Navestigio merges everything into one timeline in your local time. Each view answers a specific question.

Case summary

What happened?

Visits, downloads, searches and credentials, with activity by day and hour and the most visited sites.

Timeline

When?

Every artifact in a single table. From any row, see what happened 5, 15 or 60 minutes before and after.

Recovery and integrity

What was deleted?

Recovers URLs from free pages, freeblocks and the -wal, and detects gaps and deletions even when nothing is left to recover.

Interpretation

What did they search for?

Extracts search terms from Google, Bing, YouTube and eleven other sites, and decodes Google Analytics cookies.

Alerts

What do I review first?

Editable rules by acceptable-use category: cloud storage, anti-forensics, malware, adult content, illegal material and more.

Delivery

What do I hand over?

A ZIP with the tables, the findings and the timeline, a manifest with SHA-256 and MD5, and checksums another examiner verifies with one command.

Detects deletion even when nothing can be recovered

SQLite leaves traces when records are deleted: counters that don't add up, gaps in ids, timestamps that go backwards. Navestigio measures them and reports them without overstating.

  • PriorityWhere to look first. Not an accusation.
  • ConfidenceHow solid the measured fact is.
  • InterpretationWhat the fact is consistent with, such as "deletion of the most recent activity".

Plus a scope block that states what was not analyzed, so a clean result is never taken at face value.

Every row says where it came from

The timeline tells a live record from a recovered one or from an indicator, with its confidence and the source of its timestamp. A deleted record is never mistaken for a confirmed visit.

Export to CSV for Excel, JSONL for Timesketch or jq, bodyfile for mactime, or l2t CSV for Plaso.

Built to handle evidence

A forensic tool downloaded from the internet has to earn trust. This is what Navestigio guarantees and how to check it.

  • Nothing leaves the computerA Content Security Policy blocks every network connection. No telemetry, no external resources.
  • Evidence is never modifiedIt reads an in-memory, read-only copy. The -wal is applied to that copy, never to the original.
  • Hashes of everythingSHA-256 and MD5 of every file opened, and SHA-256 of every file exported.
  • Hostile evidence under controlQueries run in a Web Worker. A malicious database is cancelled without freezing the page.
  • Safe exportsCSV files neutralize formulas so a malicious page title does not run in Excel.
  • Verifiable buildEvery change rebuilds the app from source, compares sql.js byte for byte with npm and runs tests in Chromium.

Get started in three steps

Nothing to install. Python is optional: it only serves the app on 127.0.0.1 so it can be installed as an app.

  1. Download and verifyGet the zip of the latest version and compare its SHA-256 with the one published in the release.
  2. Open NavestigioDouble-click iniciar_navestigio.bat, or open navestigio.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The first time, a guided tour shows you the essentials with sample data.
  3. Load the evidenceDrag the files in or open the profile folder. Navestigio recognizes each artifact.
certutil -hashfile navestigio-3.5.0.zip SHA256      # Windows
sha256sum -c navestigio-3.5.0.zip.sha256           # Linux or macOS

Supported artifacts

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based browsers, Firefox and Safari.

History

History, places.sqlite and Safari's History.db: visits, transitions, searches, downloads with their redirect chain.

Credentials and forms

Login Data and Web Data: saved credentials (counts encrypted passwords) and autofill.

Cookies

Chromium and Firefox cookies, with Google Analytics, BIG-IP and Quantcast decoding.

Quick navigation

Omnibox Shortcuts, Top Sites and Favicons with their thumbnail.

Profile

Bookmarks, Preferences, Secure Preferences and Local State: accounts, extensions and profiles.

Edge

Edge Collections (collectionsSQLite).

Sessions

Firefox sessionstore.jsonlz4: open and closed tabs, with their last-used or closed time.

Extensions

Chromium Extensions folder and Firefox extensions.json, with sensitive permissions highlighted.

Deletions in Safari

Safari tombstones prove that history was deleted, and for which date range.
